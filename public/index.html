▲ DIVERGENCE — altitude is high while pressure falls. This is the configuration that precedes a bust. It is not a sell order.

Percentiles rank each reading against this instrument's own record — "90th percentile" means the gauge has stood here or higher on 10% of observed days, not that anything has a 10% chance of happening. That record contains only a handful of genuine stress episodes, and days within an episode are not independent observations. Zone bands sit at the 20th/50th/75th/90th percentiles. Marks flagged * are computed from the sub-indices that existed at the time — partial, not fabricated, and not dropped. Historical percentiles are point-in-time: each date is ranked only against what preceded it, so the backtest is never flattered by hindsight.
