    MELT-UP DETAIL

    CREDIT CANARY — LADDER

    Stress travels up the ladder. Until it reaches investment grade it is a high-yield problem, not a systemic one — and the stage is held below CONTAGION while IG is calm for exactly that reason. Data limit: FRED licenses the ICE BofA tier indices on a rolling ~3-year window, so CCC, BB and IG history begins around 2023 and the ladder cannot be read through 2008 or 2020. Broad HY reaches further back.

    RATES · CREDIT · EQUITIES

    The sequence this chart exists to catch: the 10Y rises, credit begins widening, equities stay elevated, then equities break. And its mirror — the 10Y collapsing while spreads explode, which would be deflationary-bust confirmation rather than relief. Hunter expects the second; Noble and Chanos point at the first. Both can happen, in order.
